Privacy Policy

Meter Replacement Committee — Last updated: September 25, 2026

Notice: This app is an independent, unofficial productivity tool. It does not represent Kuwait's Ministry of Electricity, Water and Renewable Energy or any government entity, and it does not claim official endorsement or affiliation.

1. Data stored locally

The app stores locally: the name entered by the user, PDF files selected for import, tasks extracted from those files, task statuses and notes, attachments added by the user, and verified or cached task-location information needed for app functionality.

The developer does not operate a backend server for storing this data. The current version does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking technologies.

2. PDFs, photos, and camera

PDFs and photos are selected by the user for processing in the app. The camera is used only when the user chooses to capture an image or scan a document. The app does not upload these files or images to a developer-operated server. Users may choose to share them using iOS sharing features.

3. Kuwait Finder / PACI and map services

When task-location lookup is used, the app sends the task’s automatic address number, a persistent randomly generated installation identifier, operating-system and client information, request time, language, and Kuwait search bounds to Kuwait Finder / PACI. Follow-up requests send the returned location record identifier. Generating a location-sharing link also sends the task’s longitude and latitude. These are task coordinates; the request fields for the device’s current location and account name are empty. Results may be cached locally. The service can also receive normal network information such as the connecting IP address.

The developer does not operate Kuwait Finder / PACI and does not control their retention or processing practices. Those services are subject to the policies and terms of their operator.

When a user chooses to open a location in an external map service, coordinates or a location link may be passed to the selected map app or service, which then processes that information under its own policy.

4. Sharing

Reports, files, and attachments are not automatically shared with other parties. Sharing occurs only when the user explicitly chooses to use an iOS share feature or opens an external service.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on the device until the user removes it using available in-app deletion controls or deletes the app and its data from the device. Deleting the app removes its local app data according to normal iOS behavior. Depending on device settings, app data may be included in iCloud or computer device backups. The app does not provide its own cloud synchronization. Deleting local app data does not delete existing device backups, exported copies, shared files held by recipients, or information retained by external services. Manage those copies through the relevant device, backup, recipient, or service controls.

6. Tracking and advertising

The current version does not use targeted advertising, cross-app or cross-site tracking, or the advertising identifier for tracking purposes.

7. Children

This app is a work productivity tool and is not directed to children.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest revision date is shown at the top of this page.
